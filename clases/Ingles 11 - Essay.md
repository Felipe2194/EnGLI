### Learning right and wrong

It's very difficult for children to understand the difference between right and wrong behaviour but is part of life and every person in their lifes has to take responsability of it and help the children figure it out on their own so they can learn and dont repeat.



> La introduccion la usamos para meter un statement y en la siguientes dos topics los explicamos y le damos sentido



The child's age can be very helpful, since we are talking about children, we can understand that much of their behavior is common to all children and reflects their childhood. Knowing that they have time to learn and are beginning to understand the things around them.



The real source of their behavior lies within the family, children often reflect their own homes and families, for this very reason, the family plays a fundamental role in teaching the child how to behave and how to treat others just as they would treat their own family members. The way a child is treated at home is the way they treat people outside the home.



The only way to learn is by making mistakes. As they explore life, they will make many mistakes, which is a good thing since they are just starting out, but it is essential that they learn from their mistakes and always try to not repeat them so they can grow and improve their behavior.



> En nuestro topico explicamos nuestra propia idea y le damos un cierre a la idea principal.



In conclusion, they are children, and we have to see them as such, passionate and curious to discover new things in life. Step by step, they will learn right from wrong, and with the help of their families and loved ones, they will be able to grow up in the best possible way.
