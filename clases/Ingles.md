**A review: A book with a interesting cover**

Like most people, I already knew the story of *Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone*, but I had never actually read the book. However, that changed when I saw a special edition in a bookstore.

The cover immediately caught my eye with its bright violet background. What attracted me the most was the set of cute drawings, detailed drawings of golden stars, magic potions, and castles. The lovely artwork made the book look so attractive that I just had to buy it.

The story is about Harry Potter, a young boy who discovers that he belongs to a family of wizards and goes to the famous Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Reading the book was great because it has many fun details and jokes that do not appear in the famous movies.

The writing is simple, fast, and very entertaining. It was fascinating to finally experience the original story that started such a huge worldwide phenomenon.

I had a wonderful time reading it, and I strongly recommend this book to everyone. Even if you already know the plot, the charming purple cover and the magical story are definitely worth it.
