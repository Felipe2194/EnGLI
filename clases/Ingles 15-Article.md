An Essay - Young people and sleep

Nowadays we can see how important having a good sleep is. A lot of research and professional recommendation is in our hands in order to have a better lifestyle and a great rest. Some professionals say that a good amount of sleep is resting for having around seven to eight hours of sleep each night and that´s a non-negotiable if you want to have good health.

Not getting enough sleep affects our lives. Every time you go to bed late, you are losing recovery and rest time that can't be replacedmade up. This situation is especially common among young people, whether because they go out partying or stay late studying.

Furthermore, a lack of sleep negatively affects school and studying. Tired students find it much harder to concentrate in class, remember new information and get a good grade.

Finally, an unhealthy lifestyle often develops when teenagers control their own schedules. Many young people spend late night scrolling on social media or playing video games instead of resting, which leads to poor daily habits and low energy.

In conclusion, while teenagers want independence, parents should still set reasonable bedtimes to protect their health, education and overall lifestyle.
